## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are individuals managing personal budgets and day-to-day expenses.
They use Expensasaurus to quickly log transactions, understand where money goes,
and make better month-to-month spending decisions with confidence.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, elegant, professional.
The interface should feel premium and composed, helping users feel in control
instead of overwhelmed.

### Aesthetic Direction
Visual direction should align with modern banking product quality, inspired by
Mercury's calm and refined financial UX.
Keep the established blue-first theme and clean card-based composition.
Avoid over-neon "crypto dashboard" aesthetics, noisy color palettes, and
high-contrast visual clutter.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity over decoration.
2. Use visual restraint with premium spacing, typography, and hierarchy.
3. Maintain trust-first finance UI patterns that feel stable and reliable.
4. Keep interactions subtle and purposeful, with minimal motion by default.
5. Preserve consistency across pages through reusable components and tokens.
